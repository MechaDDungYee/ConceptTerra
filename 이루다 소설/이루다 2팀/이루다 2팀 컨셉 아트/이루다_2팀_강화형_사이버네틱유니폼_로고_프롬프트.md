# 이루다 2팀 강화형 사이버네틱 유니폼 및 로고 프롬프트

## 강화형 방향성

이 버전은 이루다 2팀의 현장 대응 및 전투 수습용 강화형 유니폼이다. 이루다 2팀은 단순히 소원을 검토하는 행정팀이 아니라, 의뢰인의 뒤틀린 소원으로 인해 생겨난 규격 외 개념물과 직접 충돌할 수 있는 부서이기도 하다. 따라서 강화형 유니폼은 일반 2팀 유니폼보다 더 강하고, 더 메카닉하며, 더 사이버네틱하게 보여야 한다.

다만 거대한 전신 로봇이나 완전한 메카 슈트가 아니라, 인간 요원이 착용 가능한 강화 사이버 슈트여야 한다. 빠른 이동, 증거 확보, 소원 수습, 개념물 제압을 모두 수행할 수 있는 형태가 기준이다.

## 이미지 생성 프롬프트

```text
Create a stronger alternate uniform and logo concept art sheet for Iruda Team 2 from the Concept Terra novel project.

Use the existing Iruda Team 2 common uniform/logo identity as the base: black and gunmetal cyber/mechanic design, electric cyan and warning amber accents, cracked wish-star and number-2 logo direction, forensic cyberpunk wish-audit atmosphere, and dark organized concept-sheet layout.

This version is a more mechanic and cybernetic upgraded field-response uniform. It is used by Iruda Team 2 members who may need to fight abnormal concept objects born from distorted wishes. It should look stronger, more dangerous, and more combat-capable than the standard design, while still belonging to the same team.

Core direction:
- More cybernetic, more mechanical, more armored.
- Not a giant mecha suit.
- A powered tactical cyber-suit with modular exoskeleton assist frames.
- Wearable by human agents.
- Allows fast movement, investigation, evidence handling, wish recovery, and direct combat.
- Built to withstand conceptual anomalies, corrupted wish effects, and abnormal concept objects.

Sheet layout:
- Large full-body front view of a generic Iruda Team 2 field-response member wearing the upgraded cybernetic uniform.
- Side view and back view showing mechanical spine support, cable rails, compact power cell, and shoulder/hip attachment points.
- Heavy-response variant with more visible armor plating and mechanical bracers.
- Administrative/light variant mini panel showing how the same design can be reduced for office wish-audit staff.
- Logo/emblem panel and sleeve patch panel using the same Team 2 identity but with a heavier industrial patch feel.
- Detail callouts for mechanical forearm bracer, cybernetic hand, reinforced boots, knee pistons, collar lock, neck data port, artificial eye interface, concept-containment cartridge, and emergency warning lights.

Uniform details:
- Base suit: matte black tactical undersuit with segmented gunmetal armor plates.
- Exoskeleton: compact mechanical assist frame along forearms, calves, spine, shoulders, and hips.
- Cybernetic parts: optional mechanical forearm sleeve, artificial eye lens, temple jack, glowing neck port, wrist scanner, reinforced cybernetic fingers.
- Field gear: concept-containment capsules, hardcase for data tablet, shock baton, restraint cable launcher, compact scanner, sealed evidence slots.
- Defensive systems: warning amber hazard lights, cyan diagnostic strips, muted red emergency seals, anti-contamination vents, signal dampeners, shock anchors.
- Boots: heavy but agile cybernetic boots with ankle pistons, magnetic anchors, and reinforced soles.
- Gloves: armored mechanic gloves with glowing fingertips, reinforced knuckles, and interface pads.
- The design should clearly look capable of fighting a warped wish-born object, not just inspecting documents.

Logo/emblem:
- Keep the stylized number 2, cracked wish star, warning circuit ring, and audit check motif.
- Make the patch version heavier and more industrial.
- Tactical embroidered patch with raised rubberized edges, scratched metal backing, cyan and amber diagnostic line accents.
- No readable text.

Mood and style:
- Dark cyberpunk forensic response unit.
- High-end anime concept art sheet.
- Detailed mechanical linework.
- Clean organized panels.
- Cold, strong, dangerous, technically advanced.
- The team should look like mad auditors who can breach a contaminated wish-disaster zone and return alive.

Avoid:
- Do not turn it into a huge robot or full mecha armor.
- Do not remove the Team 2 logo identity.
- Do not use Iruda Team 1's gold lightbulb motif.
- Do not make it too colorful.
- Keep black and gunmetal dominant with cyan, amber, and small red accents.
- No readable text.
- No watermark.
- No signature.
- No extra named characters.
- No fantasy robe.
- No ordinary business suit.
```

## 강화형 디자인 고정 규칙

- 강화형은 규격 외 개념물, 변질 소원 현장, 오염된 의뢰 공간에 투입되는 장비다.
- 일반 유니폼보다 메카닉 프레임, 외골격 보조 장치, 강화 브레이서, 사이버웨어 연결부가 더 많이 드러난다.
- 제4국 기술 제휴 흔적이 일반형보다 더 강하게 드러나야 한다.
- 인간이 입을 수 있는 장비여야 하며, 거대 로봇이나 완전한 전신 메카 슈트가 되면 안 된다.
- 2팀의 차가운 행정성과 맛이 간 광기는 유지하되, 전투 수습팀으로서 강한 인상을 준다.
- 1팀의 전구/금색 소원 별 문양과 절대 혼동되지 않도록 한다.
